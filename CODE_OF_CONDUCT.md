# Code of Conduct

Be rigorous, direct, and evidence-based. Critique interfaces, behavior, evidence, and claims; do not attack people.

Reproduction failures, negative results, and boundary corrections are welcome. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private or identifying material are not accepted. Conduct reports that cannot be public should use the route documented at https://github.com/mmjbds/mianzhang.org/blob/main/.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.
